Privacy Policy — #SAMO GPS

Last updated: November 21, 2026

#SAMO GPS is a GPS speedometer and 0-to-target acceleration timer for scooters, motorcycles, and cars, published by Scooterarmy. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Location data

The app uses your device's GPS to show your speed, time acceleration runs, and record ride logs (distance, duration, top speed). Location data is processed on your device only. It is never sent to us, never sent to any server, and never shared with any third party.

Data stored on your device

Ride logs and timed-run logs are stored only in the app's storage on your own phone. Clearing them in the app, or uninstalling the app, deletes them. We have no access to them and no copy of them.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Assisted GPS

To get a faster first fix, the app may use your phone's network-based (cell tower / Wi-Fi) location while GPS is still locking. That coarse position is used only on your device to show an "ASSISTED" status. It is never sent anywhere, and speed and timing always come from GPS alone.

Permissions

The app requests precise location (required for the speedometer), coarse location, network state, and wake lock (to keep the screen on while riding). No other permissions are requested.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
